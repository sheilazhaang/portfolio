AI-Powered Mortgage Underwriting Engine

Team AI product prototype

A team-built prototype for self-employed mortgage applicants that turns complex financial documents into a structured, policy-grounded underwriting recommendation.

The problem

Mortgage underwriting still depends heavily on manual document review. Self-employed applicants make that work especially difficult because their income can vary, deposits may not come through payroll, and applications often include complex business tax forms. Our team focused on reducing that review burden without hiding how the system reached its recommendation.

Our solution

We designed an end-to-end workflow that extracts fields from borrower documents with Amazon Textract, normalizes nine document types into a shared schema, and routes the application through a 34-node decision engine. Nine RAG nodes retrieve relevant Fannie Mae policy sections so the system can return a recommendation with an explanation and supporting guideline references.

  • A borrower portal adapts the document checklist to the applicant's business structure.
  • Real-time upload states show which documents are missing, processing, or verified.
  • An underwriter workstation runs the pipeline and presents verification results, metrics, and the final recommendation.

Prototype impact

  • ~95% extraction accuracy demonstrated in prototype testing
  • 3-minute target for an initial eligibility decision
  • 83% estimated reduction in processing cost per loan

The time and cost figures are prototype projections from the project presentation, not production results.

Demo

Technology

  • Amazon Textract for query-based OCR and confidence scoring
  • FAISS with BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings for policy retrieval
  • Groq API with Llama 3.3 70B for guideline-grounded explanations
  • A shared Python schema contract connecting extraction and decisioning
